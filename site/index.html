Find My Cube

Hidden cubes, found like a lost phone. AVION2 leads you to an area, and the moment the game shows a cube near you it switches to precision finding: distance, height and which way to turn.

Optional and off by default. It uses cube positions the game sends to your PC before the cube is on your screen, which can be seen as an unfair advantage, so AVION2 asks you to confirm before switching it on.

  1. Search the area. Reported cube spots are rough, so the compass gives you an area to walk or fly around instead of a fake distance.
  2. Spotted. The game shows the cube: the compass turns green with the exact distance, how high it is and whether to turn left or right.
  3. Opened. Opening it is detected by itself and the compass moves on to the next one. Cubes that others open first are noted too.

Everything in one window

Live meter

An overlay on top of the game with damage per player, crits, back attacks, damage taken and the boss's HP. A low-HP alert with its own sound, and a warning when the game server freezes. Optional HUD effects draw on the game itself: your HP bar blinks when low, and the boss's skills show under its health bar as it casts them.

Fight analysis

Every fight is saved: damage and healing per skill, crits, back attacks, damage taken by source, a timeline of each player's casts and buff uptime. Lag spikes are marked where they happened.

Fight replay

Watch any fight again from above: where everyone stood, who hit whom, and what each player was doing at that moment. Scrub through it, change the speed, click anyone for their HP and last skill.

Dungeon runs

Every sealed dungeon, expedition, stronghold and instance is timed from entering to leaving: boss kills with their fight time, deaths, party, Kinah and loot. Your best time per dungeon, each boss compared with your best run, and a live clock while you're inside. Past runs can be read from your recordings.

Map

Every zone with teleports, gathering spots, monsters with their drops and NPCs. You and the players around you appear on it, with the way you face.

Timers

Spacetime Rifts, Beritra air raids, PvP events, resets, Reshanta world bosses and field boss respawns, in your time zone, with notices before they start.

Gear Forge

What it costs to enchant or amplify any item: average and unlucky materials, Kinah and attempts per level. It knows your equipped gear and what your Enhance Stones and Kinah cover.

Two themes

An AION2 look and the VOID theme. Skill and monster names in 10 languages.

Passive and private

AVION2 only reads the game's network traffic on your PC, the way a network monitor does. It doesn't touch the game, doesn't inject anything and doesn't send your data anywhere. Fights, recordings and settings stay on your PC.

Install

  1. Install Npcap (the capture driver Wireshark uses).
  2. Download the latest release and unzip it anywhere.
  3. Run AVION2.exe, press Start capture, play.

New versions show up as a banner in the app and install with one click, with a progress window.
