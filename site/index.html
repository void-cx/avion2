Find My Cube

Hidden cubes, found like a lost phone. AVION2 leads you to an area, and the moment the game shows a cube near you it switches to precision finding: distance, height and which way to turn.

Optional and off by default. It uses cube positions the game sends to your PC before the cube is on your screen, which can be seen as an unfair advantage, so AVION2 asks you to confirm before switching it on.

  1. Search the area. Reported cube spots are rough, so the compass gives you an area to walk or fly around instead of a fake distance.
  2. Spotted. The game shows the cube: the compass turns green with the exact distance, how high it is and whether to turn left or right.
  3. Opened. Opening it is detected by itself and the compass moves on to the next one. Cubes that others open first are noted too.

Everything in one window

Live meter

An overlay on top of the game with damage per player, crits, back attacks, damage taken and the boss's HP, with the skill the boss is winding up and the time left, and "Interrupted" when your party stops it. While you are in a party, everyone's HP shows too, also when they are out of view. A low-HP alert with its own sound, and a warning when the game server freezes. Optional HUD effects draw on the game itself: your HP bar blinks when low, the boss's skills show under its health bar as it casts them, a gathering's or craft's outcome shows before the bars finish, the next chain skill shows with the time left to use it, a red mark beside the boss bar shows while the boss targets you, and while flying you see how many seconds you have left.

Fight analysis

Every fight is saved: damage and healing per skill, crits, back attacks, damage taken by source, a timeline of each player's casts and buff uptime. Stagger shows how much each player's hits filled the boss's gauge and whose hit broke it, and dodges are counted per player. Lag spikes are marked where they happened.

Fight replay

Watch any fight again from above: everyone moving as the game moved them, who hit whom, where the boss's skills were about to land, and what each player was doing at that moment. Scrub through it, change the speed, click anyone for their HP and last skill.

Dungeon runs

Every sealed dungeon, expedition, stronghold and instance is timed from entering to leaving: boss kills with their fight time, deaths, party, Kinah, loot and the potions you used, and whether you left through the exit. What an expedition's reward cube gave and how close each guaranteed reward is. Your best time per dungeon, each boss compared with your best run, and a live clock while you're inside. Past runs can be read from your recordings.

Gathering and crafting

The game decides whether a gathering or a craft succeeds the moment it starts; the success and fail bars only play it out. AVION2 shows the outcome right away, above the game's panel: will succeed, will succeed with the better item, or will fail. For crafts it also tells whether the combo (two of the better item) will work, since a failed combo leaves you with nothing. A fourth abort within 5 minutes gets gathering blocked for a minute or more: AVION2 warns you before the abort that would trigger it, and counts the block down. The crafting calculator in the Gear Forge lists every material an item takes, down to the gathered ones, with what your bag already holds, and a log keeps each of your crafts with what it used and made.

Map

Every zone with teleports, gathering spots, monsters with their drops and NPCs. You and the players around you appear on it, with the way you face. Your party shows too, also out of view, with the zone each member is in; a click shows them on their map.

Timers

Spacetime Rifts, Dimensional Invasions, the Shugo Festival, the Airspace Battle, PvP events, resets, Reshanta world bosses and field boss respawns, in your time zone, with notices before they start. Bosses show the game's own pictures, also in notices and on the map.

Characters

Combat power, item level, worn titles, gear, skills, Daevanion boards and stats of your characters and of anyone you look up, from the official AION2 site. Your characters show with their portraits from there. Right-click a player in the meter to open theirs, and Compare puts one of yours beside them, piece by piece.

Legions

The legions seen on your server compared by their members' combat power or by members, the top three on a podium and your own legion marked. Click one for its members with class, item level, combat power and when AVION2 last saw them. It counts what AVION2 saw around you, in your party and in your lookups, and takes each legion's real size and leader from the game's legion list when you open it: the official site has no legion ranking.

Gear Forge

What it costs to enchant or amplify any item: average and unlucky materials, Kinah and attempts per level. It knows your equipped gear, ranks its next enchant steps by item level per Kinah with the stats each one adds, and shows what your Enhance Stones and Kinah cover. Beside it: the crafting calculator, and a stat calculator that shows what your attributes and deity stats convert into, filled in from your character.

Progress

Home keeps track of what the game counts for each of your characters: your item level (gear plus Daevanion boards), Kinah gained today against the daily limit, Abyss Points this week, tickets and keys, Daeva Pass XP, XP per hour while levelling, and your pet souls: per pet kind the souls toward the next pet or level and the monsters that dropped them, with a short notice for each soul.

Group finder

While you sit in a group finder room, Home shows it: the dungeon, its state and every slot with role, class, item level, combat power and ready check, and the join requests while you lead. Notices tell you when someone asks to join, when the room is full and everyone ready and when the dungeon starts. In the party frames a mark shows who the boss is targeting.

Two themes

An AION2 look and the VOID theme. Skill and monster names in 8 languages. A streamer mode shows every player, character and legion name as a stand-in, for streams and screenshots, and an OBS overlay puts the meter, the boss's casts and your character card on stream as a Browser Source. Your Discord profile can show your map, your dungeon run or the boss you fight.

Passive and private

AVION2 only reads the game's network traffic on your PC, the way a network monitor does. It doesn't touch the game, doesn't inject anything and doesn't send your data anywhere. Fights, recordings and settings stay on your PC. AVION2 only goes online to ask the official AION2 site, for the characters you open and for your own characters' portraits.

Install

  1. Install Npcap (the capture driver Wireshark uses).
  2. Download the latest release and unzip it anywhere.
  3. Run AVION2.exe, press Start capture, play.

New versions show up as a banner in the app and install with one click, with a progress window. What changed: see the changelog.
